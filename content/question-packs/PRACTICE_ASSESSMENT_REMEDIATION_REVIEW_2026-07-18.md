# Practice assessment remediation review — 18 July 2026

## Decision

No practice assessment is ready for publication without an attributable independent subject-matter review. The previous 100,000-question numerical pack must not be restored: it reused a very small set of number-substitution patterns across unrelated exams and did not establish assessment-specific syllabus coverage.

One bounded replacement artifact is now technically ready for reviewer intake:

- `grade-3-mathematics-practice`: 200 original MCQs in `octamy-grade-3-mathematics-practice-v1.jsonl`, now truthfully titled **Grade 3 Arithmetic, Time and Perimeter Practice** within the artifact.
- Eight narrow topics, 25 questions each: addition and subtraction within 1,000; multiplication with factors through 10; exact division within 100; missing-number addition/subtraction equations; halves and fourths of quantities; elapsed time in minutes; and perimeter by adding side lengths. This is not a full Grade 3 mathematics curriculum claim.
- The production blueprint draws four division questions and three from each other topic. The pool therefore provides at least five non-overlapping rotations for every topic and meets the 200-question global minimum.
- Contract validation: 200/200 valid, 200 unique prompts, 200 unique content hashes, zero duplicate source IDs, zero duplicate content rows.
- Governance status: **review candidate, not publishable**. Import must remain `pending` and inactive until a named independent reviewer records item-level decisions and a representative-attempt/accessibility review.

The bank is intentionally original. The NCERT textbook is used only to establish scope; no textbook exercise is reproduced.

## Reviewer protocol for the Grade 3 candidate

The independent reviewer must record a decision for every item, not only a bank-level checkbox:

1. Recalculate the answer without relying on the stored explanation.
2. Confirm exactly one option is correct and each distractor is a credible Grade 3 error, not an arbitrary number.
3. Confirm vocabulary, number range, time demand, and context are age-appropriate and culturally neutral.
4. Confirm the topic and difficulty labels match the actual skill.
5. Reject ambiguous unit wording or a prompt that needs an unavailable diagram.
6. Complete at least five representative attempts and verify no repeated question within an attempt, topic quotas, scoring, keyboard/screen-reader operation, and mobile rendering.
7. Record reviewer identity and timestamp through the normal review workflow. Do not backfill or fabricate reviewer evidence in SQL.
